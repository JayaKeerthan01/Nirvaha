Essential Evacuation Guidelines
🎒 Emergency Go-Bag
Pack 3 days of prescription medications, clean water (2L per person), dry rations, copies of government ID, torch/flashlight, and power bank.
⚡ Home Safety Shutdown
Before departing, shut off main electrical breakers and close LPG gas cylinder valves. Secure ground-floor windows and move valuables to higher elevations.
🚗 Transit Advisory
Never drive or walk through moving floodwaters. Stick strictly to highlighted arterial bypasses and yield right of way to responding emergency convoys.
📻 Communications
Keep phones on battery saver mode. Tune into local civil defense frequency 102.9 FM or SMS alerts. In life safety emergencies, dial 108.
